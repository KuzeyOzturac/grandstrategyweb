# Vision

A modern grand-strategy sandbox: use trade, diplomacy and military pressure to pursue a national objective. Pause and speed controls; fronts and objective orders inspired by HOI4; negotiable crises inspired by Victoria 3.
First scenario: Syria and its regional relationships, 1 January 2012. History supplies the starting context; outcomes diverge immediately. The game is not a historical casualty model or a recreation of any other game's assets.
Single-player desktop browser, static hosting, no accounts. Global atlas with a regional simulation. No LLM dependencies. No guaranteed historical events. Rules explain outcomes.
Complete connected loops take priority over weapon-level detail. Military costs, civilian harm and domestic support constrain expansion. Negotiated results are real successes.
