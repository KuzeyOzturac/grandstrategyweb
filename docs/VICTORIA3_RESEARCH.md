# Victoria 3 research and adaptation

User direction, 2026-10-16: prioritize national management and country interactions; retire HOI4-style army micromanagement. Supersedes the original first-build army UX. This is inspiration for an original modern setting, not a recreation of Victoria 3's assets or its complete rules.

## Mechanics coverage

| Area                  | Useful design principle                                            | Implemented adaptation                                                                                           | Remaining depth                                                                     |
| --------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------ | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Country interaction   | Geography is an entry point into foreign affairs                   | Left/right click opens country dossier, relations, commitments and actions; keyboard Enter works                 | Global countries outside the regional model are inspectable only                    |
| Diplomacy             | Relationships and commitments constrain choices                    | Persistent improvement/rivalry missions, influence capacity, agreements, sanctions, guarantees, aid              | Subject autonomy and detailed treaty articles                                       |
| Diplomatic plays      | Demands develop through bargaining before escalation               | Opening/bargaining/countdown phases, acceptance reasons, compensation, backing, obligations, backdown, ceasefire | Separate counter-demands and multiple negotiated war goals                          |
| Production            | Investment changes what the economy can supply                     | Six facility types, regional location, queue/priority/cancellation, production methods                           | Ownership classes, profits and autonomous private investment                        |
| Markets               | Prices reveal shortages and guide action                           | Four goods with supply/demand/prices; surplus-backed directional routes; embargo and maritime interruption       | Full input-output chains, competing wage markets, market unions                     |
| Population            | Material conditions should drive politics                          | Aggregate living standards, employment, radicals and loyalists respond to prices, tax and reform                 | Individual population cohorts, wealth tiers, migration, cultures and qualifications |
| Interest groups       | Economic change alters political power                             | Four groups with clout/approval, coalition majority and legitimacy                                               | Parties, elections and political leaders                                            |
| Laws and institutions | Reforms have supporters, opposition, administration and time costs | Four reforms with explicit support, debate progress, costs and bureaucracy                                       | Institutions with multiple investment levels, full constitutional change            |
| Technology            | Research changes future options                                    | Three research paths, institutes, education bonus, automated production unlock                                   | Diffusion, innovation competition and a larger research tree                        |
| Warfare               | Direct resources and strategy; commanders execute                  | Automatic deployments/combat; advance/defend/standby; readiness, supply, mobilization and civilian costs         | Admirals, amphibious operations and more nuanced front balancing                    |
| Journal and events    | Give decisions context and visible consequences                    | Shortage/reform/research attention list, periodic choices, weekly trend history and dispatches                   | Long branching journal chains and historical event libraries                        |
| Power and influence   | Economic relationships can translate into political power          | Influence reserved by missions/obligations, infamy for new wars, trade dependence visible through routes         | Custom power blocs, bloc principles and subject politics                            |
| UI lenses/tooltips    | Explain both available actions and constraints                     | Map overlays, live acceptance preview, disabled reasons, state construction and country hover hint               | Nested/pinnable tooltips and richer map lenses                                      |

Nineteenth-century colonization, slavery laws and period-specific institutions are not suitable defaults for a 2012 scenario. Modern equivalents require their own design, rather than copying names. No claim is made that every Victoria 3 mechanic is implemented.

## Official sources consulted

Retrieved 2026-10-16. Foundational diaries describe design intent at their publication dates; they are not treated as an exhaustive description of the latest patch.

- Paradox, [Dev Diary 57: The Journey So Far](https://www.paradoxinteractive.com/games/victoria-3/news/dev-diary-57-the-journey-so-far), 2022-08-31. Society/economy/politics as the central player loop; commander execution. Used to move attention away from stack movement and toward national decisions.
- Paradox, [Dev Diary 54: Trade Revisions](https://www.paradoxinteractive.com/games/victoria-3/news/dev-diary-54-trade-revisions), 2022. Market-to-market price incentives. Our bounded routes use actual surplus and explicitly report zero-volume/suspended states; they do not reproduce Victoria 3's trade formulas.
- Paradox, [Dev Diary 80: Law Enactment and Revolution Clock](https://www.paradoxinteractive.com/games/victoria-3/news/dev-diary-80-law-enactment-and-revolution-clock-in-13), 2023. Political change as a contested process. Adapted as deterministic support/legitimacy-based progress with visible opposition costs.
- Paradox, [Dev Diary 58: Interest Revisions](https://www.paradoxinteractive.com/games/victoria-3/news/dev-diary-58-interest-revisions), 2022. Strategic attention limits diplomatic reach. The regional campaign deliberately does not pretend every global country has a full simulation.
- Paradox, [Dev Diary 44: Battles](https://www.paradoxinteractive.com/games/victoria-3/news/dev-diary-44-battles), 2022. Generals and fronts resolve battles. Adapted through a strategic controller over existing deterministic movement/supply rules.
- Paradox, [Dev Diary 38: Trade Routes & Tariffs](https://www.paradoxinteractive.com/games/victoria-3/news/dev-diary-38-trade-routes-tariffs), 2022. Economic policy connects trade and domestic effects. Adapted as bureaucracy costs and an open-trade reform with tariff tradeoffs.
- Paradox, [Dev Diary 75: Diplomatic Improvements](https://www.paradoxinteractive.com/games/victoria-3/news/dev-diary-75-diplomatic-improvements-in-1-2), 2023. Negotiated demands should not be a decorative prelude to mandatory war. Backdown, acceptance and ceasefire remain working results here.
- Paradox, [Sphere of Influence / Power Blocs, official Steam announcement](https://steamcommunity.com/games/529340/announcements/detail/4185611434351815137), 2024. Reviewed for longer-term bloc and economic influence work; full blocs are deferred, not represented by inactive buttons.
