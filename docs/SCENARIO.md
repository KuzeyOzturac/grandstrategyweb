# Scenario: Fault Lines, 1 January 2012

Historical anchors only: Syrian protests began March 2011; armed opposition emerged in 2011; Arab League observer mission was present across the turn of 2012. An aggregate opposition actor is a gameplay abstraction of disparate groups, not a claim of unified command. Later territorial entities are not present by default.
Starting control pockets, units, stockpiles, diplomatic scores, fiscal accounts, routes and political values are balanced gameplay approximations. Whole-area opposition control overstates the precision of early insurgent control. Map data is contemporary administrative geometry, not a verified 2012 boundary reconstruction. These caveats appear in onboarding and the dossier.
